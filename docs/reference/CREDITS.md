# Image credits

## Keke photograph

The passenger home and rider-details screens use a cut-out of a Bajaj RE auto-rickshaw.

- Source: [Bajaj RE.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bajaj_RE.jpg) on Wikimedia Commons
- Author: [Calreyn88](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:Calreyn88)
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Changes: the museum background was removed, the vehicle was cropped, darkened slightly, and given a soft ground shadow. The cream body panels and canopy were recoloured toward Keke Yellow (`#FFC629`); the black body, wheels, and glass were left as photographed. The files are `packages/ui/assets/keke/`.
